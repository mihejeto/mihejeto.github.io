Honestly

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

This is for you if

  • You've noticed changes and nobody else wants to name them
  • There are siblings, and they disagree
  • Your parent still has capacity and a strong opinion
  • You've been putting the conversation off for months

This isn't for you if

  • There's an emergency happening this week — call us instead
  • Capacity has already been lost and decisions are legal now
  • You're looking for a list of facilities
  • You want someone to make the decision for you

Questions

Before you apply.

Can my brother or sister attend too?

Yes — one price per household, bring whoever needs to be in the room. In fact it works better when the sibling who disagrees is there.

What if I can't make the live session?

You get the recording and the written materials. But the live Q&A is where most people get their actual answer, so come if you can.

Is this going to turn into a sales pitch?

No. If you want to work together afterwards that conversation is available, but the workshop is the thing you paid for and it stands on its own.

Why is there an application instead of a buy button?

Because this doesn't suit everybody, and 24 places go quickly. Two minutes of questions means nobody pays for something that was never going to help them.
