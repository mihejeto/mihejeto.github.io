I work with independent professionals who are genuinely good at what they do and almost invisible online. I build what they sell, the page that sells it, and the system that books the calls.

I don't charge a fee. I take a share of the new revenue it creates. If it creates none, I'm paid nothing.

Who this is for

You've spent years becoming the person people call when something has gone wrong. There are reviews with your name in them. Clients who found you by word of mouth and stayed.

And none of that is working while you sleep. Everything you earn is tied to an hour you personally showed up for. Meanwhile someone with a fraction of your experience is outranking you, because they published and you didn't.

That's the gap I work in. Not marketing — a second way for your expertise to earn, built by someone else, running in the background.

How the partnership works

01
We agree what you earn today
Written down before anything starts. That number is yours and I'm never paid a penny of it. I'm only paid on what we add on top.
02
I build the offer and the system behind it
What gets sold, the page that sells it, the booking flow, the follow-up, the tracking. You give me one filming session and the knowledge in your head. You never open an editor or a spreadsheet.
03
You approve everything before it exists publicly
Nothing goes out with your name on it that you haven't read and agreed to. If the first piece isn't right, you walk away and you've lost a morning.
04
I'm paid a share of what it brings in
A percentage of new revenue, tracked lead by lead, for a fixed term. No retainer, no setup fee, no minimum, nothing up front.
Why I work this way
Anyone can promise you results while invoicing you monthly regardless. Taking a share of revenue means I carry the same risk you do — if I build the wrong thing, I've worked for nothing. It also means I'll tell you when an idea of mine isn't worth filming, because I'm not paid for activity.

How you'll know what I actually did

A revenue share is only fair if both of us can see the same numbers. So the counting isn't done from memory or at the end of the month — it's built into the system from the first click.
